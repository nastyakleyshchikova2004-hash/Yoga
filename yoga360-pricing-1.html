01 — Project Overview
Concept & Market Position

Yoga 360 offers immersive yoga retreats hosted in a private villa on the Mediterranean coast of Northern Cyprus. The shared villa model delivers a premium boutique feel at a fraction of the cost of traditional per-room hotel booking. The wellness retreat market in Cyprus ranges from $380 to $3,300 per person; Yoga 360 occupies the premium value segment with two clearly differentiated formats targeting different audiences and booking windows.

Marketing & Agency Budget

Express: €200 per run — lighter digital campaign targeting local and regional audiences. Split per person: €17 at 12 pax.
Agency Partnership Model
Fixed retainer / campaign fee per retreat run — not a per-person commission. This protects margin as group size grows. Recommended: 1–2 agency partners on a revenue-share or fixed-fee basis per season.

Agencies can be upsold on branded content and photography from the retreats.

06 — Key Assumptions
Financial Notes for CFO
01
Villa Model
One private villa for the whole group at €300/night. Pool, sun loungers, fruit garden on-site. Public beach nearby. This is the core cost advantage — per-person accommodation drops to €210 (Signature) vs €50 (Express) at base group size.
02
Food Budget
€30/person/day for full board: red fish & salmon, chicken, local greens, seasonal vegetables, and fresh fruits for smoothies. Northern Cyprus local markets deliver premium Mediterranean quality at low cost.
03
Transport
Minivan hired at €40/day. For Signature (7 days): €280 total (€28/person). Covers daily beach trips and mountain excursions. For Express (2 days): €80 total (€7/person).
04
Marketing & Agencies
Fixed per-run budget: €400 (Signature) and €200 (Express). Covers advertising agencies and social media campaigns. Model is fee-per-run — not per-person commission — protecting margin at full capacity.
05
Seasonality
Active season: March–November (10 months). Peak demand: April–May and September–October. Off-peak (July–August heat) can be filled with early-bird pricing at 10% discount with minimal margin impact.
06
Express as Funnel
Express (€300) is the entry point. Expected 20–30% conversion to Signature. Each converted participant adds €867 profit. Running Express 2× per month drives both standalone revenue and Signature pipeline.
